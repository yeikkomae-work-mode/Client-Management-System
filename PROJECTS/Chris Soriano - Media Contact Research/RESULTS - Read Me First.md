## Honest hit rate

Against the 30–40% projected in the estimate:

- **12 of 74 (16%)** — genuine dedicated press contact or PR firm
- **~40%** — general email or media-routed form (usable, lower odds)
- **~30%** — phone only, or no published media route at all
- **6** — blocked, pending a 2-minute manual browser check

The projection was optimistic. Asian parent companies publish far fewer press
contacts than US consumer brands, and the mainland Chinese and Vietnamese
state-linked firms publish almost none. **This is why Tier 3 was not recommended** —
those 42 companies are drawn from exactly the categories performing worst here.

## Recommended next step

Do **not** buy Tier 3 yet. Run the 12 strong targets plus the HK walk-ins first,
see who replies, then decide. The 6 manual checks are the cheapest value on the
board — 15 minutes for what could be 4–5 more real press contacts.
