to an outside PR firm.

## 3. Pilot findings — 6 companies researched before quoting

I ran the real workflow on 6 companies spanning easy/hard before estimating. Results matter,
because they change what can honestly be promised:

| Company | Dedicated press email? | What actually exists |
|---|---|---|
| Kikkoman USA | **No** | Web form only. No email published anywhere. |
| Kinjirushi USA | **No** | Web form + phone. |
| Lee Kum Kee USA | **Partial** | General email + form with a "Media Enquiry" option. |
| Gekkeikan USA | **No** | Phone + address only. |
| Red Boat | **Partial** | `sales@` only. Press page lists coverage, no contact. |
| Suntory | **Likely yes** | Real media-contact page exists, blocks scraping — needs a manual browser open. |

### The critical finding

AI search tools confidently returned **`pr@kikkoman.com`** and **`media@kikkoman.com`**
for Kikkoman. Both are **fabricated** — neither appears anywhere on any Kikkoman property.
Same pattern with `support@gekkeikan-sake.com` for Gekkeikan.

This is exactly the failure Chris is trying to avoid, and it confirms the job has to be done
by hand with a source URL logged against every single row. Every address in the deliverable
gets a source link so he can spot-check any of them.

### Realistic hit rate

Based on the pilot, expect roughly:
- **30–40%** — a genuine dedicated press/media email or a named PR firm
- **40%** — a media-routed web form, or a general email that is the only real route
- **20–30%** — no published media route (phone, or a non-English parent-company form)

Japanese and Chinese corporates in particular publish far fewer direct emails than US
consumer brands do. Chris's instinct ("usually it's a PR firm or they'll say for media
inquiries go here") holds well for US-facing brands and much less well for the Asian parent
companies on this list. **Bouncing a bad list is worse than a short list**, so where there is
no press email I log the best real route rather than inventing one.

## 5. Deliverable format

Google Sheet, one row per company:

`Company · Country · Category · Website · Best Media Contact · Contact Type ·
Named Contact/Title · Source URL · Confidence · Notes`

- **Contact Type** — dedicated press email / PR firm / media web form / general email / phone only
- **Source URL** — the exact page the address came from, so any row can be spot-checked
- **Confidence** — Verified (seen printed on the page) vs Needs manual check
- Nothing goes in without a source. No pattern-guessed addresses.
