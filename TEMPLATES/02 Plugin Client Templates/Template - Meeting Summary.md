## HOW TO FILL IN

**Summary:** Read through transcript, bullet the main topics, then write 2-3 connecting sentences.

**Action Items:** Look for:
- "Can you...?"
- "We'll need to..."
- "By next week..."
- Anything with a timeline

**Key Decisions:** Look for:
- "We decided to..."
- "We're going to..."
- "New approach..."
- Changes from the plan

**Blockers:** Look for:
- "We need to figure out..."
- "The issue is..."
- "We can't move forward until..."
