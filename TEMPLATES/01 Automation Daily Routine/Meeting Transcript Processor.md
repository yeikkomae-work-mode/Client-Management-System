# Meeting Transcript Processor

**Use this:** When you have a meeting transcript (from phone recording export, Teams, Zoom, etc.), paste it here.

---

## HOW TO USE

**Step 1:** Paste the transcript in this format:

```
MEETING TRANSCRIPT

Client: [Chris Caffera / Chris Drew / Yoni / etc]
Date: [Date & time of meeting]
Attendees: [Names of people on call]
Platform: [Phone / Teams / Zoom / Google Meet / In-person]

[Paste full transcript here]
```

**Step 2:** I will process it and return:
- Summary (2-3 sentences of what was discussed)
- Action items (who, what, by when)
- Key decisions
- Blockers identified
- Next steps

---

## EXAMPLE INPUT

```
MEETING TRANSCRIPT

Client: Chris Caffera
Date: 2026-08-04, Monday 10:00am PHT
Attendees: Chris Caffera, Fatin, Eikko
Platform: Teams

TRANSCRIPT:
Chris: "Hi everyone, let's review last week's campaign performance."
Fatin: "The Apollo campaign had a 12% open rate and 8 replies. Good start."
Eikko: "I've prepared the Hubspot metrics. Open rate is 10%, 5 replies. Lemlist had 6% opens."
Chris: "Great. Fatin, can you write 5 new email subject lines by Wednesday?"
Fatin: "Sure, I'll have them by EOD Tuesday actually."
Chris: "Perfect. Eikko, can you set up the follow-up sequence by end of week?"
Eikko: "Yes, I'll complete it by Thursday."
Fatin: "We should also test the LinkedIn post timing. Can we do that this week?"
Chris: "Good idea. Eikko, can you schedule 3 tests this week?"
Eikko: "Yes, I'll schedule them for Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday at different times."
Chris: "Next meeting Monday 10am again. Let's review the sequence results then."
```

## WHAT I'LL EXTRACT

**Summary:** 2-3 sentences capturing the core discussion

**Action Items:** Each item includes:
- What needs to be done
- Who's responsible
- Due date (if mentioned)

**Key Decisions:** What the team decided to do differently

**Blockers:** Any issues or problems discussed

**Next Meeting:** When and what to review

**Notes:** Any other important context

---

## FORMATS I ACCEPT

- Phone recording transcript (copy-paste from wherever you export it)
- Teams transcript (Ctrl+Shift+A in Teams chat for automatic transcript)
- Zoom transcript (exported from Zoom recording)
- Google Meet transcript (auto-generated in Meet)
- Manual notes (if you wrote them yourself)
- Conversation log (if you copied the chat)

**Just paste it with the header "MEETING TRANSCRIPT" and I'll process it.**

---

## STORAGE

After I process it:
- Summary is stored in your client's record
- Action items are added to your task list
- Next meeting date is noted
- Full transcript is archived for future reference

When you ask for "good morning" recap tomorrow, I'll include:
- Any action items due today
- Reference to the meeting
- Reminders of decisions made
