## The Purpose

Every role in this ecosystem exists for one reason: **put qualified, interested, ready-to-talk prospects in front of the closing team so the closing team can close the deal.**

---

## Lead Temperature — Cold / Warm / Hot

- **Cold** — knows little/nothing about Penji. Goal: earn attention, create curiosity/relevance. Not selling yet.
- **Warm** — engaged, evaluating, has real questions. Goal: remove doubt by answering the questions they have (and haven't) voiced.
- **Hot** — convinced but hasn't committed. Goal: make it easy to say yes — friction kills hot prospects, speed/clarity/confidence close them.

**Cold → Warm:** specificity beats volume; match the hook to the channel (email leads with pain point, <100 words; social DM leads with a genuine observation + question; cold call gets to the point in 30 seconds); keep the ask small (a response, not a demo); lead with social proof.

**Warm → Hot:** listen before pitching; diagnose fully before prescribing a plan/price; surface objections early ("What concerns do you have about making a change like this?"); make value concrete and specific to them, not abstract.

**Hot → Close:** assumptive close ("Let's get you set up — I'll send the link now"); choice close (two good options, not yes/no); urgency close only when genuine urgency exists (never manufacture it); remove every remaining friction point before it's asked about.

**Inbound vs. Outbound:** inbound prospects already raised a hand — respond within 5 minutes, qualify/diagnose on the first call, match their energy, aim to close within 1–2 conversations. Outbound prospects didn't ask for contact — never pitch on the first touch, personalization isn't optional, most outbound closes happen on touch 4–6, and a hot outbound prospect needs one more layer of validation before the close than an inbound one does.

**Universal closing principles:** confidence is non-negotiable; the prospect's own words close the deal, not yours; silence after a close-ask is a tool — stop talking and wait; every follow-up promised must land exactly when promised.

## The Sales Ecosystem — Three Pillars

| Pillar | Channel | Reaches | Produces |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Email Outreach** | Inbox | ICP at volume | High-volume warm-reply flow |
| **Social (LinkedIn) Outreach** | LinkedIn / Twitter / Instagram, 10 persona accounts | ICP where guard is lowest | Trust-first warm conversations |
| **Win-Back + Website Leads** | Multi-channel / inbound signals | Former customers + self-identified interest | Highest-converting re-engagement + highest-intent new prospects |

**Coordination standards across all three pillars:**
- **Do-Not-Contact List** — no prospect worked by more than one pillar at once
- **Handoff standard** — warm signals reach the closing team within minutes, with full context (who, source, what they said, what they know, recommended approach)
- **Intelligence loop** — results from every pillar (response patterns, objections, conversions) feed back to improve the other pillars continuously

The closing team isn't downstream of the ecosystem — every targeting/messaging/prioritization decision across all three pillars exists to give them the best (not most) meeting opportunities, and their feedback shapes the ecosystem back.
