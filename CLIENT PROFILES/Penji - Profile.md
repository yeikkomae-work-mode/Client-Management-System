**Role:** Agency Advisor — Outbound Outreach Specialist, Agency Listing track (confirmed via "Agency Advisor Eikko" Notion doc, Aug 13, 2026)

**The Objective:** Build and maintain the most complete database of digital marketing agencies globally, enrich every record with verified contact data, and run coordinated email + LinkedIn outreach until every qualifying agency has been contacted, responded, or exhausted the sequence. Agencies are Penji's highest-LTV segment.

**Monthly KPIs:**
- 25+ meetings scheduled
- 10% response success rate
- 5+ total accounts closed

**Core workflow:**
1. Source agencies (Clutch, Agency Spotter, UpCity, Design Rush, GoodFirms, G2, Sortlist, The Manifest, Bark.com, plus LinkedIn/Google Maps/social/state registration searches)
2. Filter by ICP — 5–200 employees, active client roster, offers social/paid/content/SEO/branding/web dev, no existing Penji subscription
3. Identify real decision-maker (founder/owner → MD → creative director → head of client services → ops director)
4. Enrich via Gojiberry (Hunter.io, then Findymail as backups)
5. Post to Slack for 👍/👎 verification
6. Load into Lemlist (email, 4 touches/12 days) + Dripify (LinkedIn, parallel) within 24hrs of enrichment
7. Route warm replies to **Joan** within 1 hour; **Oliver** closes
8. Submit weekly report every Friday

**Tasks:**
- Grow the agency master database every week without exception
- Maintain enrichment, sequencing, and CRM/tracking sheet in real time
