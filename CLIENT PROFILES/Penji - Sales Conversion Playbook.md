This is the **closing-team layer** of the funnel — the consultation call script and the pre-/post-call email automation run through SavvyCal. It sits downstream of the Agency Advisor / outbound work tracked in `Penji - Profile.md` and `Penji - Agency Advisor Quick Reference.md`: outbound generates the warm reply/meeting, this playbook is what happens once that meeting is booked and run. Companion doc: `Penji - Sales Ecosystem & Team Standards.md` (the org-wide lead-temperature framework, outreach pillars, and current team rules/goals).

---

## The Goal

- Deliver the best sales experience the prospect has ever had while converting consultation calls into paying Penji customers **ON THE SAME CALL**
- Turn a prospect's design problem into a production, financial, or competitive problem
- The job: Ask → Listen → Understand → Confirm → Show Proof → Close
- Make an offer at the end of every call

## Pre-Call Email Workflow (automated through SavvyCal)

**Email 1 — Initial acknowledgment** (on booking)
Subject: *Your Consultation Call with Penji has been approved!*
Confirms the call, previews the 3 things the demo will cover (design bottlenecks, Penji workflow, how leading companies use Penji), includes a Coinbase quote, and asks the prospect to accept the calendar invite + save the Google Meet link.

**Email 2 — Introduction to Penji / expectations** (sent 2 days before the call, from Oliver)
Subject: *An Introduction to Penji*
Sets expectations: submit any creative request, dedicated team works fast with daily updates, unlimited revisions, download when ready. Links to "Why Penji?" (25k+ brands).

**Email 3 — Case studies** (sent 1 day before)
Subject: *See How Teams Use Penji*
Uber, CVS, 1-800-Flowers case studies + Trustpilot/G2 links.

**Email 4 — 1-hour reminder**
Subject: *Meeting in 1 Hour* — meeting link + time.

**Email 5 — 5/10-minute reminder** *(new)*
Subject: *Friendly reminder…* — meeting link.

## Pre-Call Preparation (mandatory)

Before every call:
- Review the Slack analysis of the call
- Know: what the company does, marketing dept size, likely design needs, possible Penji use cases by industry
- Case studies pre-loaded by industry
- Run the savings calculator ahead of time so the number is ready
- Have one case study ready where you personally interviewed the client

## Consultation Call Flow

1. **Intro (1 min)** — something light to crack a smile
2. **Opening (1 min)** — recap what you found in your pre-call research on their business, ask "Did I miss anything?"
3. **Initial discovery question** — *"Can you walk me through your specific creative process and what that looks like?"* (or one of the alternates: current process/what they need help with, how they get assets done, what brought them to Penji)
4. **Time-based discovery questions** — how many people do creative work (part-time/full-time/freelance); hours/week reviewing work; hours/week revisioning; hours/week designing
5. **Dig-deeper prompts** — "Tell me more about that." / "Where does the bottleneck happen?" / "What happens when designs get delayed?" / "How often does that come up?" / "What would an ideal setup look like?" / "Why did you sign up with [past provider]?" / normalize the problem ("that's one of the most common things we hear...")
6. **Strategic pause (5 sec)** — visibly write down their answers
7. **Success recap (critical)** — *"Let me make sure I have everything right before I show you anything,"* then recap 3–5 bullets in their exact words, wait for explicit confirmation ("Yes"/"Exactly"/"That's right") before moving on
8. **Pants-on-fire question** — *"If we solved this for you, what would it do for your business?"* — turns the design problem into a production/financial/competitive problem
9. **Personalized walkthrough** — screen-share only what solves their specific problem, using a customer account that matches their business. Typical flow: Brand Folder → Create Project → Upload References → Revisions Tool → Daily Updates → Art Director/PM if needed
10. **Trust & change-management response** *(use if they've been burned before / distrust the process)* — explains the brand profile is built before any project starts, and if the first designer isn't the right fit, the brand profile carries over to the next one — no starting over
11. **Plan recommendation** — *"Based on everything you shared — especially [pain points] — the plan that typically works best is the [plan name]."* Then: *"Do you think this is 100% going to work to get you to [goal]? What specifically is holding you back?"*
12. **ROI calculator** *(new)* — show live on the call, or offer to send by email if they're not comfortable sharing costs live
13. **Ending the call** *(new: ask how they found Penji before closing)* — two versions: (a) promise a recap email with examples, case studies, a matched customer interview, and AD recommendations, plus offer a no-pressure 15-min follow-up touchpoint; (b) if they need to check with someone (e.g. their CEO), get a specific follow-up date and push for a **call** follow-up (with the decision-maker + an Art Director on it) rather than an email
14. **Final question** — "What's the one thing that would make Penji feel right for you without another sales meeting?" and *(new)* "Are you ready to start, or looking at other options?" (surfaces competitor mentions — e.g. Flocksy's freelancers, DesignPickle's monthly hour stock — to address directly)
15. **Offer / continuation** — never end the call without a clear next step and an offer made; exhaust all options first ([offers reference](https://app.notion.com/p/31102afd984c805a826cc0d6fce9a520?pvs=21))

## Post-Consultation Email Workflow (11-day cycle, new)

- **Email 1 — same day recap:** a custom plan built from what they said on the call (tailored features, price locked until an expiry date), plus 4–5 matched case studies. Sample used Johnathan (Co-Founder, Penji) as sender.
- **Email 2 — +2 days if no reply (AD call):** sent 24–36 hrs after the call for Agency/Creative Team/Custom plans that include an Art Director — shares 2–3 specific observations the AD made reviewing the prospect's public brand presence, reiterates the still-locked offer.
- **Email 3 — +2 days if no reply:** a matched case study with a measurable result, plus a couple of active social proof links; reiterates the still-locked offer.
- **Email 4 — +2 days if no reply:** leads with the 30-day money-back guarantee — full refund, no questions asked.
- **Email 5 — +2 days if still no reply:** final, low-pressure — "reply and tell me where you're at, no hard feelings, no follow-up (no promises)."

## 15–60 Day Post-Consultation Chain

Ten emails (Day 15 → Day 60), rotating through: what Penji does in plain language → social proof (G2/Trustpilot/customer stories) → a matched case study → the 30-day guarantee framed as risk removal → a "typical week with Penji" walkthrough → an honest in-house-vs-freelance-vs-agency-vs-Penji comparison → a time-limited 20%-off-first-3-months offer → a "day one" onboarding walkthrough → plan pricing explained plainly (Starter $499/mo, Growth $995/mo, Pro $1,995/mo, or Custom) → a final "door is still open" close-out email.

**60+ day chain:** folds into ongoing email campaigns (not yet detailed beyond that).
