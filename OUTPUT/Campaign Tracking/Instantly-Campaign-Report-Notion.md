## Timeline / Duration Note

> Instantly's UI doesn't expose an exact per-campaign start date (Schedule tab just shows "Start: Now," and the Activity log only gives relative timestamps like "a month ago"). Based on the 12-month analytics charts and activity logs, all 12 live/completed campaigns show sending activity concentrated in a short burst roughly a month before this report (approximately early-to-mid July 2026) — consistent with them being built and launched together as one batch.

**Planning takeaway:** once a campaign goes live, the bulk of send volume and replies land within roughly **1–2 weeks** of launch, even though the "Completion %" badge can stay below 100% for a while if leads are still mid-sequence. For the Plusvibe side (Mortgage Brokers + 2 new targets), a similar 1–2 week ramp to first-pass completion is a reasonable planning assumption, with full completion trailing a bit longer depending on step wait-times (3–4 days between steps, per the sequences already built).
