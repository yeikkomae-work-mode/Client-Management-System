1. **AI-sounding language.** Spintax dashes (` - `) standing in for em dashes: he called it "a dead giveaway AI." Also flagged the repeated "automotive aftermarket brands" phrasing showing up multiple times in a single email.

4. **Should not be about Q4.** Original drafts leaned entirely on Q4/holiday-gifting-rush framing. Removed.
5. **Review every campaign, not just the one walked through.** All three (Cosmoprof, Global Pet, AAPEX) are revised below, not just the one flagged live.
