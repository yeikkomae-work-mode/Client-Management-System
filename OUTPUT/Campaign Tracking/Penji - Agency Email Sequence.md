# Penji — Agency Cold Email Sequence

4-touch Lemlist sequence, 12 days, per Agency Listing training standard. Every send picks **one** primary angle based on the agency's segment (see angle guide below) — don't mix angles within a single sequence run for one agency.

Merge tags used: `{{firstName}}`, `{{companyName}}`, `{{icebreaker}}` (agency-specific observation pulled during Gojiberry/manual research — required, never leave generic).

---

## Angle Guide — pick one per agency based on signal

| Angle | Use when the agency is... |
|---|---|
| **Capacity** | Growing fast, can't hire designers quickly enough, job posting signal detected |
| **Margin** | Buying design at high cost from freelancers/other vendors, marking it up thin |
| **Freelancer reliability** | Visibly dependent on freelance designers (portfolio credits, LinkedIn mentions of "our freelance team") |
| **White-label** | Wants to offer design as a service without building an internal design team — smaller agencies, generalist shops |

## Email 1 — Day 1 (the opener — no pitch, one specific observation)

**Subject:** quick one, {{firstName}}

**Capacity angle:**
```
Hey {{firstName}},

{{icebreaker}}

Eikko
```

**Margin angle:**
```
Hey {{firstName}},

{{icebreaker}}

Question for you — when {{companyName}} needs design work done, are you handling that in-house or outsourcing it out per project?

Eikko
```

**Freelancer reliability angle:**
```
Hey {{firstName}},

{{icebreaker}}

How's your design bench looking these days — still leaning on freelancers to cover client work, or has that gotten easier to manage?

Eikko
```

**White-label angle:**
```
Hey {{firstName}},

{{icebreaker}}

Do you ever get client requests for design work that's outside what {{companyName}} normally builds in-house?

Eikko
```

## Sequencing Notes (per training standard)

- Dripify LinkedIn connection request fires **in parallel** with Email 1 — short, specific note, no pitch.
- Email opened but no reply after 48 hours → LinkedIn message fires as second-channel touch (see Multi-Channel Outreach training).
- LinkedIn connection accepted → message within 24 hours referencing the email.
- Every reply — positive or negative — gets logged and Slack-flagged per the 👍/👎 standard from the training doc.
- Booking link only appears in Email 2 onward, never Email 1 — first touch starts the conversation, doesn't try to close it.
- A/B test one variable per week (subject line, icebreaker style, angle performance) — required per role standard, results go in the Friday report.
