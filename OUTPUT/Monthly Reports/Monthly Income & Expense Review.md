# Monthly Income & Expense Review

**Automated monthly financial summary with profit per client.**

---

## HOW TO USE THIS SYSTEM

### Weekly (As You Work)
**Log hours in your work logger** (already doing this)
- Each task entry includes hours spent
- Hours automatically attributed to client

### As You Have Expenses
**Tell me or log the expense:**

Example ways to log:
- "Expense: Hubspot subscription 1,500 PHP"
- "Expense: Office supplies 800 PHP"
- "Spent 500 PHP on internet today"
- Add to end-of-day report: "Expenses: 2,300 PHP (tools)"

**I'll track automatically.**

### End of Month (Or Start of Next Month)

**Say: "monthly income & expense review"**

I will:
1. ✅ Pull all billable hours per client
2. ✅ Calculate revenue:
   - Chris Caffera: hours × $7 USD
   - Yoni: hours × $5 USD
   - Chris Soriano: hours × $7 USD
   - Chris Drew: $200 AUD (fixed)
3. ✅ **Auto-convert to PHP:**
   - Check current USD→PHP rate
   - Check current AUD→PHP rate
   - Calculate totals
4. ✅ Subtract all expenses (you provide in PHP)
5. ✅ **Calculate profit per client:**
   - Revenue - Expenses = Net profit
   - Profit margin % per client
6. ✅ Generate full report
7. ✅ Save to file
